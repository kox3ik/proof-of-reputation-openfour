How it works04

One score, one fee curve, every launch.

01 / SCORE

History becomes a score

Volume, activity, holding time, verified identity and past rug participation make a score from 0 to 1. The weights are set by the protocol.

02 / FEE

The score sets the fee

OpenFour reads the wallet that signs the trade and prices it. Trusted wallets pay less. Fresh and flagged wallets pay more. No call before sending, no change in terminals.

03 / DEPOSIT

Low scores post a deposit

A wallet with a low score leaves a refundable deposit on its buys: 6.4 % at score 0, shrinking smoothly to zero at score 0.4. It comes back after honest trading. If the wallet joins a rug, the deposit burns into the scam fund.

04 / PENALTY

Rug wallets pay on every trade

The penalty grows with the wallet’s rug component, up to 6.4 % on buys and sells. It can still buy and sell. Selling is never blocked, only priced.

05 / CASHBACK

Scammers pay honest wallets

Penalties and forfeited deposits fill the scam fund. Honest wallets get a cashback and a bonus from it, never more than they paid in fees.

06 / PROTOCOL

The protocol fee is untouched

OpenFour’s own fee stays exactly as it is. The module adds a second, reputation-based layer on top of it.

Price, not permission07

We don’t close the door. We price it.

Permissioned launches

Papers, freezes, caps

  • KYC documents before you can trade
  • Caps and freezes decided by an operator
  • An operator who can freeze your funds
  • Honest traders leave, volume drops
Proof of Reputation

Every trader stays

  • No documents, only on-chain history
  • Nothing is blocked, selling is never blocked
  • No freeze: a flag changes the price
  • Scammers pay, honest wallets get paid back
Limits, honestly08

What this is not, yet.

Scores come from our service

Scores are published by our service, signed, with an open rule. Anyone can recompute them from public data. They are not computed on chain, and ZK proofs are on the roadmap.

Testnet only

Everything here runs on BSC testnet. Identity tokens are stand-ins there. Nothing was run on an official, audited OpenFour registry.

AI-reviewed, not audited

The code was reviewed by several AI models and fixed after each round. That is not a human audit.

Transfer-collection rugs

Rugs that collect tokens by plain transfers into one wallet are not detected yet. A transfer fee prices them before and after migration, but the detector does not flag them.
